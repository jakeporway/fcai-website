Apply

Apply to use our computers

This short application takes about ten minutes. Answer in your own words. You don't need a technical answer to every question, and if you're unsure about something, just say so.

We read every application and aim to reply within a few weeks. We look for projects that serve the public, teams that can do the technical work, and a responsible plan for handling data.

FAQ

Common questions

Don't see yours? Email team@foundationcivic.ai.

Who can apply?

Nonprofits, government agencies, university research groups, and fiscally sponsored projects, in the US or elsewhere. What matters is that the work serves the public and that you can handle data responsibly. You don't need a referral.

Does it really cost nothing?

Yes. There's no charge for computing time or for advice from our experts. Your team's own time is the main investment.

We're not very technical. Can we still apply?

You'll need at least one person who can work with code and data, because your team runs its own work on our computers. If that's not you yet, the private AI tools we're building may be a better fit. Join the early-access list.

What if our project outgrows what we're given?

Tell us early. Capacity is limited, so we can't promise more, but we can help you scale the work down, run it in a different order, or plan a move to paid computing.

What happens to our data when the grant ends?

You'll have a set amount of time to copy everything off our computers before your access ends. Plan for that from the start, and never keep the only copy of anything on our cluster.
