How it works

A grant of computing power, with experts to help you use it

Think of it as a grant. Instead of money, you receive time on powerful computers built for AI, plus expert help to use them well. Most grants run for up to a year.

The program is in its pilot stage, so we work with a small number of organizations at a time and keep improving as we go. Many of our grantees are referred by funders like the Bezos Earth Fund, but anyone can apply.

What you get

Everything you need to get started

01

Time on our computers

Your team gets dedicated use of part of our computing cluster, usually 8 GPUs, and more if the project calls for it.

02

Help getting set up

Cornell Tech's IT team creates your accounts and walks your team through connecting securely. We provide step-by-step setup guides.

03

Expert advice

Our data scientists and AI engineers help you plan the work and use the machines efficiently, with check-ins throughout your grant.

Why expertise matters

Why we pair computers with expert help

Running AI on powerful hardware is a skill of its own. In our experience, teams new to it can use up donated computing time two to ten times faster than an experienced commercial team would.

A few conversations with someone who has done it before can save weeks of work and make a grant stretch much further. That's why every grantee has access to our experts, not just our machines.

Is it a fit?

What our computers are built for

Our computers are built for creating and improving AI models, and for running them over large amounts of data: for example, teaching a model to recognize crop diseases, analyzing thousands of hours of camera footage, or studying genetic data.

They aren't meant for hosting a website or an app that runs around the clock. If you're not sure whether your project fits, apply anyway. We'll give you a straight answer.

For technical teams: workload guide
Inference on small and medium modelsStrong fit
Full fine-tuning of ~13B-parameter models, or LoRA on larger onesLikely fit
Full fine-tuning of ~30B models; larger training runsDepends on capacity
Full fine-tuning of 70B+ modelsUnlikely to fit
Hosting a live app, website, or always-on serviceNot a fit

Good to know

What to expect

  • It adds to what you already have. Our computers should supplement your existing tools, so keep a backup plan for anything critical.
  • Support is reliable, not instant. Technical questions go through Cornell Tech's help desk and usually get an answer within about a week.
  • You'll need someone technical on the team. Your team works directly on a shared Linux system, connecting through a secure VPN.
  • Your data stays yours. You're responsible for it, so always keep your own copies.

Step by step

From application to results

  1. Apply

    Tell us about your organization and the problem you're working on.

  2. Talk it through

    We have a short call to confirm the project is a good match for our computers.

  3. Get set up

    Cornell Tech creates your accounts and walks your team through access.

  4. Build

    Up to a year on the cluster, with check-ins and advice from our experts.

  5. Wrap up

    Move your working model to your own systems, or apply to extend for a second term.
